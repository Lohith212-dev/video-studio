## Tools
- [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code) by Anthropic: runs the pipeline.
- [HyperFrames](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes) by HeyGen: renders HTML + GSAP animation to MP4.
- [Kokoro-82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M): the free local voice (Apache 2.0).
- [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org): audio mixing and video joins.
- [Rough.js](https://roughjs.com): the hand-drawn look.
- Optional: [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai) for the ElevenLabs v3 voice and AI images.
