# Brief

- **Topic:** How a wealth manager's customer-service AI plan is priced, tracked and accounted for.
- **Source:** the user's call mind dump + three infographics (tech landscape for context only, CX AI strategy & 12-month budget, cost-centre cheat sheet).
- **Audience:** executives and managers in financial services considering AI for customer-facing teams.
- **The one thing to remember:** an AI strategy only counts when it's priced, tracked monthly, and has explicit scale / improve / stop decisions.
- **Goal:** explain (thought leadership).
- **Length:** about 3 minutes, 16:9.
- **Must include:** 100,000 questions/yr, $12 each, 60% repetitive, $1.2M = $0.7M + $0.4M + $0.1M, monthly budget vs actual, scale/improve/stop, 5 cost centres.
- **Tone:** calm, measured, no hype.
- **Look:** style `ray-dalio-principles`, brand `demo` at `accent` strength.
