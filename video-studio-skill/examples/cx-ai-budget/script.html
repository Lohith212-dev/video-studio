Script — A Strategy Without a Budget Is a Wish

1. Title options

  1. A Strategy Without a Budget Is a Wish (used; paid off by hook A at 0:00-0:11)
  2. Pricing an AI Plan Before Spending a Dollar
  3. The Twelve-Dollar Question: Funding AI in Customer Service

2. Premise

A wealth manager's service team answers a hundred thousand questions a year at twelve dollars each, and six in ten are repeats, but an AI plan with no budget behind it is only a wish, therefore the plan was built as a priced, tracked machine that can be scaled, improved or stopped.

3. Video type, framework, and why

Explainer with data-story evidence. Spine: Dalio principle → mechanism → evidence → implication. The user's own thesis is a principle, the viewer is a skeptical business audience who needs to see cause and effect, and the source is a system (work, cost, tracking) rather than a sequence of events. Devices: one open loop, But/Therefore turns, signposting, principle recap.

4. Format and length

Long-form 16:9. Target 180 seconds at 130 wpm (from the Dalio style brief) = about 390 words. Actual: 391 words, 188.5 seconds estimated (+5%, within tolerance).

5. Audience

General business viewers: executives and managers in financial services who are considering AI for customer-facing teams. They know budgets and service teams; they do not know AI tooling or capitalisation rules. At the end they should understand that an AI plan needs a priced budget, a reserve, monthly tracking and explicit decision points.

6. Hook options

  • A. Principle (used): "Here is a principle. A strategy that is not backed by numbers, and checked against them, is not a strategy. It is a wish."
  • B. Surprising number: "Every time a customer of this wealth manager asks a question, it costs twelve dollars. They ask a hundred thousand times a year."
  • C. Question: "What does it really cost to bring AI into a customer service team, and how would you know if it was working?"

11. Open questions (for the user)

  1. Is the $1.8M benefit per year, or over about two years? ROI 50% and a 16-month payback only add up if it is about two years. Current default: the figure is left out; a ready line is in Parked material.
  2. Are the client figures (especially $1.161M actual spend) real, and may they be shown publicly with the client unnamed? Current default: client anonymized as "a wealth manager"; actuals described as "in the tracker".
  3. What should viewers do at the end? For example, book a call or download the budget template. Current default: no hard CTA; the close is "Start with the budget. Then choose the tools."
